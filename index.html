Terms & Conditions

Full Cancellation Policy

Full refund for cancellations made within 48 hours of booking, if the check-in date is at least 14 days away. 50% refund for cancellations made at least 7 days before check-in. No refunds for cancellations made within 7 days of check-in.

Minimum Stay Rules

A minimum stay of 3 nights is required for all seasons. During peak summer months (July-August), a 5-night minimum may apply.

House Rules

No smoking inside the villa. No parties or events. Quiet hours from 23:00 to 08:00. Maximum occupancy of 6 guests must be respected.

Payment Terms

A 30% deposit is required via bank transfer to secure the booking. The remaining balance is due 14 days prior to arrival. Security deposit of €500 is required upon check-in (fully refundable).
